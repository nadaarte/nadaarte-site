03 / The artist

HELLO,
I'M WENWEN.

Oil painter / Valencia, Spain

I'm Wenwen Cai, a contemporary painter inspired by landscapes, people, colour and the memories we carry from place to place.

Through expressive colours and brushwork, I explore the emotional life of everyday surroundings — from quiet woodland paths to bright coastal landscapes.

My work is rooted in observation, imagination and the joy of seeing the world differently.
